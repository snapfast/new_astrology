In Vedic astrology, planetary transits—known as **Gochar**—refer to the continuous movement of planets through different Zodiac signs (*Rashis*) relative to your natal Moon or Lagna.

## Understanding Hora (Planetary Hours)

Every day is divided into 24 planetary hours called **Horas**, starting from local sunrise. Each Hora is ruled by one of the seven classical planets in a specific sequence based on speed.

- **Sun Hora**: Great for administrative tasks, speaking with authority figures, and leadership.
- **Moon Hora**: Ideal for emotional reflection, family matters, and public relations.
- **Mars Hora**: Favorable for physical exertion, competition, and courageous actions.
- **Mercury Hora**: Excellent for writing, business negotiations, learning, and mathematical work.
- **Jupiter Hora**: Optimal for spiritual practices, financial investments, higher education, and legal counsel.
- **Venus Hora**: Perfect for creative arts, romantic endeavors, buying jewelry, and social activities.
- **Saturn Hora**: Good for discipline, organization, long-term planning, and land investments.

Combining knowledge of current Gochar transits with daily Hora selection allows you to align your daily actions with cosmic timing.
