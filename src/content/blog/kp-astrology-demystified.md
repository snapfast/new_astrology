Krishnamurti Padhdhati, popularly known as **KP Astrology**, is an advanced system developed by the late Prof. K.S. Krishnamurti. It refines classical Vedic astrology by placing primary emphasis on Nakshatra Sub-Lords rather than just Sign Lords or Rashi.

## Key Principles of KP Astrology

- **Placidus House Cusp System**: Unlike equal house systems, KP uses the Placidus house system where house cusps start at exact unequal degree divisions based on geographical latitude.
- **Star Lord and Sub-Lord**: Each Nakshatra (13°20') is further subdivided into 9 unequal parts proportional to the Vimshottari Dasha period of the planets. The ruler of this subdivision is called the **Sub-Lord**.
- **KP Prashna (Horary)**: When birth details are unknown or uncertain, a horary number between 1 and 249 can be selected to cast a chart and answer specific single-point queries with uncanny precision.

## Why Sub-Lords Are Crucial

While the Sign Lord indicates the general nature of an event and the Star Lord shows the result, the **Sub-Lord determines whether the event will actually manifest or fail**. This removes ambiguity and provides definitive answers to life questions.
